# Campaign Strategy Brief & Audience Insights
## Brand: Aashirvaad (Foods - Staples & Organic Nutrition)
**Parent Company:** ITC Foods | **Theme:** Festive Diwali

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Brand Purpose
Aashirvaad is an iconic market leader in India, driven by the core values of Uncompromised Purity, Maternal Love & Nourishment, Traditional Chakki Grinding, Digestive Wellness. 
Under the campaign theme **"Festive Diwali"**, the objective is to drive high-impact emotional resonance, boost brand recall, and trigger immediate digital / retail conversion.

### 2. Core Brand Taglines & Philosophy
- **Primary Tagline:** "Shuddhata Ka Asli Aashirvaad"
- **Alternate Taglines:** "Happy Tummies, Healthy Families", "Rotiyan Itni Soft Ki Haath Lagao Aur Toot Jaaye", "100% Pure Chakki Atta with 0% Maida"

### 3. Key Hero Products & Value Propositions
- **Aashirvaad Shudh Chakki Atta**: 100% whole wheat, traditional 4-step sorting, 0% maida
- **Aashirvaad Select Sharbati Atta**: 100% MP Sharbati wheat, golden grain, stays soft for hours
- **Aashirvaad Svasti Pure Cow Ghee**: Special slow-cook process for golden granular aroma
- **Aashirvaad Organic Dals & Pulses**: 100% certified organic, chemical-free farm traceability

### 4. Target Audience Profiles & Pain Points
- **Segment:** Health-Conscious Homemakers (28-50 yrs, Tier 1/2/3 pan-India)
  *Mindset & Desires:* Committed to family health, zero compromise on grain purity & softness
- **Segment:** Fitness & Clean Eating Seekers (24-40 yrs, Urban)
  *Mindset & Desires:* Focus on high-fiber, gut health, millet superfoods, organic staples
- **Segment:** Young Working Couples (25-35 yrs, Metros)
  *Mindset & Desires:* Quick cooking convenience without sacrificing traditional home taste

### 5. Signature Sensory Triggers & Visual Guidelines
- **Sensory Triggers:** Aroma of freshly roasted wheat, Super soft 3-finger roti tear, Warm puffy steam rising, Wholesome golden grain texture
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Sun-drenched golden wheat fields, rustic earthenware, traditional stone chakki textures, steaming hot fluffy puffed rotis with glistening golden ghee, warm family dining sunlight.
- **Primary Palette:** `#E5A93C` | **Secondary Palette:** `#2E7D32`

### 6. Historical Performance Benchmarks
- **Display Banner CTR:** 0.19%
- **Video View-Through Rate (15s):** 42.1%
- **Social Story CTR:** 1.10%
- **Top Historical Converting Angles:** Roti staying soft after 6 hours in lunchbox, 100% Whole wheat purity demonstration, Mother's blessings & happy gut smiles

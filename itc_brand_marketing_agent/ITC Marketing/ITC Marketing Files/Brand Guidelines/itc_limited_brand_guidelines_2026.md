# ITC Limited - Corporate Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Vision, Mission & Philosophy
ITC Limited is guided by a core philosophy: **"Nation First: Sab Saath Badhein"** (Together, let's grow). This philosophy represents ITC’s commitment to building a competitive, sustainable, and profitable Indian enterprise that creates immense societal value.

*   **Slogan**: *Enduring Value*
*   **Vision**: To sustain ITC’s position as one of India’s most valuable and respected corporations while delivering sustainable value to all stakeholders.
*   **Mission**: To provide world-class performance across Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG), Hotels, Paperboards & Packaging, Agri-Business, and Information Technology.

## 2. Core Visual Identity
ITC's corporate visual identity represents trust, heritage, and modern forward-looking growth.

### 2.1 Corporate Name Formatting
*   **Official Name**: **ITC Limited**
*   **Rule**: Never write the corporate name with full stops (do not write "I.T.C. Limited"). The full stops were officially removed in 2001 to signify ITC's successful transition from a tobacco-focused company to a highly diversified conglomerate.

## 3. Logo Usage & Specifications

### 3.1 Clear Space Rules
To ensure the ITC logo retains its strong visual impact, always maintain a minimum border of empty space around it.
*   **Measurement Standard**: The clear space is measured by a square equal to the height of the letter **"I"** in the ITC logo. 
*   No other text, page borders, or distracting graphics should ever cross into this designated boundary.

### 3.2 Minimum Sizing
To preserve legibility across diverse mediums:
*   **Print**: Minimum horizontal width must be **30mm**.
*   **Digital Screens**: Minimum horizontal width must be **125 pixels**.

### 3.3 Background & Color Rules
*   **Solid White or Light Backgrounds**: Use the full-color deep blue logo (`#062F62`).
*   **Dark or Patterned Backgrounds**: Always use a single-color, solid white knockout logo to maintain perfect contrast and readability.
*   **Never**: Modify the logo colors, squeeze/stretch the proportions, or place text inside the clear space boundary.

## 4. Brand Architecture (Endorsed Brand System)
ITC uses an **Endorsed Brand Architecture** to manage its massive product portfolio. This system ensures that while individual product brands maintain their unique consumer personalities, they inherit trust and credibility from the parent corporation.

### 4.1 Product-to-Parent Guidelines

| Product Brand | Primary Channel / Target | Role of Parent Brand (ITC) | Packaging Placement Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aashirvaad** | Staples, Spices, Ghee | Represents purity, trust, and quality. | Endorsed with a subtle "An ITC Brand" mark on the back/front of packaging. |
| **Sunfeast** | Biscuits & Baked Indulgence | Represents quality ingredients and delight. | Endorsed with "A Quality Product from ITC" or the ITC logo near the net weight. |
| **Fiama / Vivel** | Personal Care & Soap | Represents dermatological safety and trust. | Endorsed with "A Quality Product from ITC" on the secondary cardboard packaging. |
| **Classmate** | Student Stationery | Represents premium standards and safety. | Endorsed with the ITC corporate logo and commitment to community initiatives. |
| **ITC Hotels** | Luxury Sustainable Hospitality | Inherits "Responsible Luxury" philosophy directly. | Uses the "ITC Hotels" joint logotype on all guest collateral. |

## 5. Copywriting & Tone of Voice
Our messaging is designed to build trust and feel deeply rooted in Indian culture, while reflecting clean, modern, and forward-thinking standards.

### 5.1 The Pillars of Our Voice
1.  **Trustworthy & Professional**: Avoid casual modern internet abbreviations when representing the parent brand. Speak as a reliable guardian of family health and premium quality.
2.  **Culturally Grounded**: Weave in local values, regional traditions, and family relationships to make the copy feel relatable to everyday Indian lives.
3.  **Sustainable & Mindful**: Highlight ITC's industry-leading environmental status (Carbon-Positive, Water-Positive, and Solid Waste Recycling-Positive) in all institutional collateral.
